Physics: Linear Motion Kinematics

Definition

Kinematics is the branch of classical mechanics that describes the motion of points, bodies, and systems of bodies without considering the forces that cause them to move.

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as

Worked Examples & Practice

Example 1: A car accelerates from rest ($u = 0$) at $2\,\text{m/s}^2$ for $5\,\text{s}$. Find final velocity $v$.
Solution: $v = u + at = 0 + (2)(5) = 10\,\text{m/s}$.


Practice Questions:
1. Find $s$ when $u=5, a=2, t=4$.
2. Find $v$ when $u=10, a=-1, s=20$.
3. Find $t$ when $u=0, v=25, a=5$.
4. Find $u$ when $v=30, a=3, t=6$.
5. Find $a$ when $u=2, v=14, s=32$.

Chemistry: Balancing, Moles & Limiting Reactants

Definition

Stoichiometry measures the quantitative relationships of reactants and products in chemical reactions based on the law of conservation of mass.

Moles ($n$) = Mass ($m$) / Molar Mass ($M$)
Gas Volume at RTP = Moles $\times 24.0\,\text{dm}^3$
AMR vs RMR = Available Molar Ratio vs Required Molar Ratio

Worked Examples & Practice

Example 1: Balance $H_2 + O_2 \rightarrow H_2O$.
Solution: $2H_2 + O_2 \rightarrow 2H_2O$.


Practice Questions:
1. Balance $Fe + Cl_2 \rightarrow FeCl_3$.
2. Calculate moles in $44\,g$ of $CO_2$.
3. Find limiting reactant when $10g$ $Mg$ reacts with $10g$ $O_2$.
4. Determine volume of $2$ moles of $H_2$ at RTP.
5. Balance $C_3H_8 + O_2 \rightarrow CO_2 + H_2O$.

Mathematics: Quadratics & Simultaneous Equations

Definition

Quadratic equations are second-order polynomial equations. Simultaneous equations are sets of equations containing multiple variables solved together.

$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
Cramer's Rule: $x = \frac{D_x}{D}, y = \frac{D_y}{D}$

Worked Examples & Practice

Example 1: Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.
Solution: $(x-2)(x-3)=0 \implies x = 2, 3$.


Practice Questions:
1. Solve $2x^2 + 5x - 3 = 0$.
2. Solve $x^2 - 9 = 0$.
3. Solve $x+y=5$ and $2x-y=1$.
4. Find discriminant of $3x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$.
5. Solve 3-variable system $x+y+z=6$.
